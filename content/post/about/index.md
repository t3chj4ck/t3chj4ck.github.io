## Story

A tree,  
growing where mountains hide the sun.

Poor soil.  
Little light.

Every spring,  
the woodpeckers come.

Every winter,  
the cold wind blows.

It grows no stronger.

Its trunk bends with the wind.  
Its bark remembers every beak.  
Its roots know the shape of stone.

Still—

it keeps  
a single leaf  
toward the light.
